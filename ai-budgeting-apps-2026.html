Managing money has never been easier — or more confusing. In 2026, a flood of AI-powered budgeting apps promises to do the hard work for you. We tested the top apps, created real accounts, and evaluated each one from a beginner's perspective. Here's what we found.

Detailed Reviews

Detailed Reviews

1. Piere — Best Overall for Beginners

Free tier available, Premium $9.99/month. The standout AI budget app of 2026. Setup takes under 5 minutes. Piere's AI immediately starts categorizing transactions across 95% of US banks. The "smart savings" feature suggests specific amounts to move to savings each week. AI chat answers nuanced questions like "Can I afford a $500 vacation next month?" based on your actual situation.

Trade-off: Free tier limits to one savings goal and 90 days of history. Enough for the first 3-6 months.

2. YNAB — Best for Zero-Based Budgeting

$14.99/month or $99/year (34-day trial). The gold standard for a decade. The "give every dollar a job" philosophy is powerful but requires active management. AI features are limited compared to Piere. Not for beginners who want "set and forget."

3. Treasury — Best Visual Dashboard

$8/month (14-day trial). Beautiful real-time charts show 50/30/20 allocation, net worth trends, and spending breakdowns at a glance. AI assistant answers natural language queries. Missing: No Android app yet.

4. Moneko — Best for Privacy

Free / Pro $5.99/month. Unique approach — no bank scraping. Tracks spending through WhatsApp messages, email receipts, and voice notes. Perfect for cash users, families, or anyone uncomfortable sharing bank credentials.

5. Copilot — Best for Apple Users

$13/month or $90/year. Apple-exclusive. Excellent investment + budget tracking in one app. AI categorization is good but occasionally miscategorizes. Missing: No Android.

Free Options

Free AI Budgeting Options

  • Piere Free: One savings goal, 90-day history, auto-categorization. Best free option.
  • Moneko Free: Unlimited WhatsApp tracking, one Pocket.
  • EveryDollar (Free Tier): Dave Ramsey app. Solid manual budgeting, no AI.
  • Google Sheets + template: Flexible, free. Our budgeting 101 guide has a free template.
