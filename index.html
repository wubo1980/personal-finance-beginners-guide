FAQ

Questions New Readers Usually Ask

Do I need to be good at math?

No. Most beginner money skills are more about systems, habits, and understanding basic trade-offs than complex calculations.

Do I need a high income to start?

No. A smaller income can make progress slower, but the core habits still matter and still help.

Which guide should I read first?

If daily spending feels messy, start with budgeting. If emergencies or debt feel scary, start with those pages instead.
